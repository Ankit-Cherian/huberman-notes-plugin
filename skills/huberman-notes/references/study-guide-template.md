## Template Usage Notes

### Frontmatter Fields

- **episode**: Episode number (integer)
- **title**: Full official title
- **guest**: Guest name or "Solo" for solo episodes
- **date_aired**: Original air date
- **duration**: Episode length
- **topics**: Array of topic tags for indexing
- **protocols_count**: Number of actionable protocols extracted
- **confidence**: 1-5 rating of how well you understood the material
- **reviewed**: Last review date

### Quality Standards

1. **Every protocol needs specific parameters** - No vague "do this regularly"
2. **Evidence levels must be honest** - Don't oversell weak evidence
3. **Include Huberman's personal practice** - Distinguish from general advice
4. **Timestamps enable re-listening** - Essential for long episodes
5. **Action items make it practical** - Study guides should lead to action

### Difficulty Ratings

- **Easy**: Behavioral changes, no cost, minimal time investment
- **Medium**: Requires some planning, moderate time/cost
- **Advanced**: Significant commitment, may need medical supervision

### Evidence Levels

- **Strong research**: Multiple RCTs, meta-analyses, scientific consensus
- **Emerging research**: Limited studies, promising but not definitive
- **Anecdotal**: Based on clinical observation or personal experience
- **Theoretical**: Mechanistically plausible but not yet tested

### Cost Ratings

- **Free**: No cost (behavioral protocols)
- **$**: Under $20/month
- **$$**: $20-50/month
- **$$$**: Over $50/month
